## Separate readiness statements

**A. Code-side readiness.** The security defects that were confirmed in source (bootstrap password, role escalation, signup over-post, suspension bypass, cross-portfolio documents) are fixed and covered by the readiness suite. Financial regression suites that exist in the repository passed without a change to the posting pipeline.

**B. Environment readiness.** Production secrets, rotation of any account created by the old bootstrap, a real backup restore, and dependency upgrades are not done.

**C. Business and compliance readiness.** Tax output is implemented software behaviour. It is not a legal or ZIMRA certification.

**D. Manual acceptance.** The owner still needs to exercise the browser workflows. That testing was not performed in this pass.
